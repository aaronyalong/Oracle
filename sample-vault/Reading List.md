# Reading List

## Read
- "Attention Is All You Need" — the original [[Transformers]] paper. Section 3.2 explains [[Attention Mechanism#Scaled dot-product attention|scaled dot-product attention]].
- "Lost in the Middle" (Liu et al., 2023) — shows LLMs use information at the start and end of long contexts better than the middle. Relevant to [[Retrieval-Augmented Generation#Common failure modes]].

## To read
- "Programming in Scala, 5th edition" by Odersky, Spoon, and Venners — covers Scala 3. See [[Scala]].
- "Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks" (Lewis et al., 2020) — the paper that named RAG.
- "Designing Data-Intensive Applications" by Martin Kleppmann.
